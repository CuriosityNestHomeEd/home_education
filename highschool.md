# High School

Homeschooling in and through High School is possible! This page is a listing of resources specifically for that endeavor. Please check with your individual state / country of residence in case there are specific rules you must follow different from younger grades for high school. Remember to keep your records, and have a transcript running of courses taken and completed. There will be some resource overlap, especially where it concerns grade 8.
