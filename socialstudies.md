<H1>Social Studies</H1>

Social Studies, including history and geography, curriculum will be listed on this page.
At this time, in no particular order. Editing, arrange, and extra details will be added
over time. Some curricula mentioned cover more than just Social Studies, others are places
to find particular lesson plans. Research and decide what is best for your own individual
needs.

- Blossom and Root - Charlotte Mason and Waldorf inspired
  - https://www.blossomandroot.com/
- Bookshark
  - Is the "secular" arm of Sonlight, which is a religous literature based curriculum.
    - https://www.bookshark.com/shop-by-subjects/reading-with-history-packages
- Build Your Library - Charlotte Mason inspired/ literature based approach to homeschooling
  - https://buildyourlibrary.com/
- Core Knowledge Online - curriculum available for K-8
  - https://www.coreknowledge.org/history-geography/
- edX
  - https://www.edx.org/search?q=&attributes=History&attributes=Social+Sciences
- Georgia Virtual Learning
  - https://gavirtuallearning.org/ga-digital-curriculum/free-resources/
- The Good and the Beautiful
  - This is a religious infused curriculum that has some free options. Especially during the 2020-2022 push for homeschooling they wanted to keep homeschooling affordable and accessible to as many families as possible.
    - https://www.goodandbeautiful.com/pages/choose-history
- Guest Hollow
  - https://guesthollow.com/shop/
- Lesson Plans on the topic of Racism from Human Rights Educators USA
  - https://hreusa.org/hre-library/topics/racism/lesson-plans/
- Pandia Press History Quest
  - Elementary and Middle School
    - https://pandiapress.com/pages/history-quest
- Pandia Press History Odyssey
  - Middle - High School
    - https://pandiapress.com/pages/history-odyssey
- Pandia Press Modern History Bridge
  - Designed for between History Quest and History Odyssey, utilizing Story of the World from Well-Trained MindGrades 3-7
    - https://pandiapress.com/collections/history-odyssey-2
- Story of the World
  - Elementary and Middle School, also used as the text of other history curricula.
    - https://welltrainedmind.com/c/series/the-story-of-the-world/
- Well-Trained Mind Press
  - History and Geography for All ages and grades
    - https://welltrainedmind.com/c/subject/history-geography/
