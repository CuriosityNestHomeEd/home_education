<H1>Mathematics Curricula</H1>

There is a variety of math curricula available today, both in textbook form and online.
Even those that teach using comic book style instruction. Below is a list, at this time
in no particular order, of math curricula.

- [Art of Problem Solving - specifically for advanced students](https://artofproblemsolving.com/store/list/all-products)
- [Beast Academy - specifically for advanced students](https://beastacademy.com/)
- [Core Knowledge K-8](https://www.coreknowledge.org/mathematics/)
- [CTC Math](https://www.ctcmath.com/)
- [edX](https://www.edx.org/search?subject=Math&tab=course&page=1)
- [The Good and The Beautiful Math](https://www.goodandbeautiful.com/pages/choose-math)
- [Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org/math)
- [Life of Fred](https://www.lifeoffred.com/)
- [Math Mammoth](https://www.mathmammoth.com/)
- [Mathseeds](https://mathseeds.com/)
- [Math-U-See](https://mathusee.com/)
- [Math With Confidence](https://welltrainedmind.com/c/series/math-with-confidence/)
- [RightStart Mathematics](https://rightstartmath.com/)
- [Saxon Math](https://www.hmhco.com/programs/saxon-math)
- [Singapore Math](https://www.singaporemath.com/)
- [Zearn Math](https://www.zearn.org)
